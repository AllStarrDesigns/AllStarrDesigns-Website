About AllStarr Designs

Local landscaping built on experience, craftsmanship, and pride in every project.

Our Story

AllStarr Designs was built on years of hands-on landscaping experience throughout Tennessee. Before starting the company, we spent nearly a decade working alongside established landscaping crews, learning the craft from the ground up.

After years of experience and three years running projects directly with a trusted partner, AllStarr Designs was created to bring that same level of professional work to homeowners across Fentress County.

Our goal is simple: create outdoor spaces that look great, function properly, and hold up for years to come.

What We Focus On

  • Landscape Design & Installation
  • French Drains & Drainage Solutions
  • Gravel Driveways
  • Property Cleanup
  • Mulch, Rock Beds & Edging

Proudly Serving Fentress County

Based in Jamestown, AllStarr Designs proudly serves homeowners throughout Jamestown, Allardt, Clarkrange, Pall Mall, and the surrounding areas.

We believe in honest work, clear communication, and delivering results that homeowners are proud of.
