Where did we Start?

Local landscaping built on experience, craftsmanship, and pride in every project.

Our Story

AllStarr Designs was built from years of hands-on experience, long days, and a commitment to doing things the right way—not the easy way. Before this company ever existed, our foundation was laid in Florida, working alongside established landscaping crews and learning every part of the trade from the ground up. From basic lawn work to full-scale landscape design and installations, those years shaped the standard we hold ourselves to today—clean work, attention to detail, and results that last. Over time, that experience turned into leadership, running projects and building something real alongside a trusted partner. But as the vision grew, so did the direction. That’s when AllStarr Designs was created—built on everything learned, but with a new purpose. Now rooted in Fentress County, Tennessee, we bring that same elevated level of professional landscaping services to local homeowners—combining strong, functional design with a sharper, more refined look that stands out. You’ll see it in our work—whether it’s mulch and rock installation, sod installation, bed redefining, or full landscape transformations. Every project is built with intention, designed to not only look great but to function properly and hold up over time. At the end of the day, this isn’t just landscaping to us. It’s building something that lasts—and raising the standard of what people should expect from a landscaping company.

Our goal is simple: create outdoor spaces that not only look great, but function properly, and hold up for years to come.
