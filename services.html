Our Services

Professional landscaping services throughout Fentress County.

Landscape Design

We design beautiful outdoor spaces that match your home and property. From flower beds to full yard transformations, our designs combine functionality with natural beauty.

Gravel Driveways

A properly installed gravel driveway improves both access and curb appeal. We grade and install durable gravel surfaces built to withstand everyday use and Tennessee weather.

Property Cleanup

Overgrown areas and neglected yards can quickly become overwhelming. Our cleanup services remove brush, debris, and unwanted growth to restore your property.

French Drain Installation

French drains redirect excess water away from your yard and foundation. This drainage system prevents standing water and protects your landscape from erosion and damage.

Sod Installation

Instant green lawns installed the right way. We prepare the soil, level the area, and install quality sod that establishes quickly and creates a healthy lawn.

Mulch & Rock Beds

Mulch and decorative rock beds help control weeds, retain moisture, and give your landscaping a clean finished appearance.
